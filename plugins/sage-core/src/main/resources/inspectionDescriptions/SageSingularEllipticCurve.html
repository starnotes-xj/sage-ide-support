Reports a Weierstrass model whose singularity follows from literal coefficients and a known base field.

The inspection handles Sage's two-coefficient short-model shorthand and five a-invariants over QQ or an explicit GF(q) when the characteristic is provable. It computes the generalized Weierstrass discriminant exactly, including characteristics 2 and 3. Dynamic coefficients or fields and unsupported models are not reported.
