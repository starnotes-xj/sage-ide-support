Reports a caret operator used for byte-wise XOR in Sage code. In Sage, ^ means exponentiation; use ^^ for XOR.

The inspection reports only unambiguous byte contexts, such as byte literals, byte-producing calls, or names annotated as bytes or bytearray. It does not flag ordinary Sage exponentiation.

When available, the quick-fix changes ^ to ^^ (or ^= to ^^=).
